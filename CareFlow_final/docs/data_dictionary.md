# Data Dictionary

## Case_ID
Unique identifier for each patient's hospital visit.

Example:
C000001

------------------------------------

## Patient_ID
Unique patient identifier.

Example:
P000001

------------------------------------

## Activity
Current hospital activity.

Examples:
- Registration
- Triage
- Doctor Consultation
- Lab Test
- Radiology
- Pharmacy
- Billing
- Discharge

------------------------------------

## Department
Department where the activity occurs.

Examples:
Emergency
Laboratory
Radiology
Accounts

------------------------------------

## Timestamp
Date and time of activity.

Example:
2026-04-06 05:36:00

------------------------------------

## Severity

Patient condition.

Possible values:
- Low
- Medium
- High
- Critical

------------------------------------

## Waiting_Time_Minutes

Waiting time before activity.

Example:
15 minutes

------------------------------------

## Status

Current activity status.

Values:
Completed
Delayed
Rework
